Elegant conceptual cover for a technical essay on the real anatomy of an AI agent, wide 16:9. Three floating sheets of translucent handmade paper are suspended vertically in a dark blue room. A single fine luminous fiber passes through each sheet, changes color at the middle sheet, reaches a quiet glowing core below, then loops back on the right. The papers suggest client context, protocol adapter and model service; the fiber suggests a request and its returned evidence. Japanese editorial minimalism, tactile paper edges, ink blue, muted amber, pale cyan and a small green accent, poetic but precise, abundant negative space, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no interface, no robot.
